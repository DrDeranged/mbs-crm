---
name: Campaign open-deal policy
description: Why archived deals do not affect campaign open-deal audience membership.
---

Campaign “open deal” audiences exclude archived deals, even if their saved stage is otherwise active. The shared active-stage definition still excludes funded, declined, dead and hold_on. This is an audience rule, not a change to archival behavior elsewhere.

**Why:** An archived historical deal should not cause a lead to be included in, or excluded from, a campaign intended for currently open financing work. Multiple qualifying deals must still produce one lead membership.

**How to apply:** Keep the archive check alongside the shared active-stage predicate. Apply it to both filtered and manually picked campaign leads, and test archived active-stage deals separately from terminal-stage deals.
